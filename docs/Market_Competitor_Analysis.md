## 1. Industry Landscape & Market Tiers

The mobile gaming market exhibits distinct monetization characteristics across geographic market tiers:

- **Tier 1 Markets (USA, UK, DE, CA)**: High eCPM (\$2.50–\$5.50), strong propensity for In-App Purchases (IAP) and subscriptions.
- **Tier 2 Markets (India, Brazil, Mexico)**: Large user volume, moderate eCPM (\$0.50–\$1.50). High growth potential for ad mediation and micro-transactions.
- **Tier 3 Markets (Indonesia, Vietnam, Philippines)**: Explosive download volume, lower eCPM. Requires optimized lightweight gameplay and high ad fill rates.
